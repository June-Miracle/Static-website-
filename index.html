PlaneClash Event: The Gathering

A one-day Modern format tournament for Magic: The Gathering players. Bring your best deck and play seven rounds for the PlaneClash Trophy.

Saturday, November 14, 2026 – MPH1 University of the East, Caloocan City

Register for the event

About the event

PlaneClash Event: The Gathering is a fan-made, fictional tournament created for a class project. It gathers 128 players for seven Swiss rounds followed by a Top 8 playoff, all played in the Modern format.

New to tournaments? A beginner table runs all day with friendly judges who explain rules, deck registration, and tournament etiquette.

Event schedule

  • 8:00 AM

    Doors open and check-in

    Pick up your player badge and submit your decklist.

  • 9:30 AM

    Players meeting

    Head judge reviews the rules and pairing procedures.

  • 10:00 AM

    Rounds 1 to 3

    Fifty-minute rounds with short breaks between them.

  • 12:30 PM

    Lunch break

    Food stalls and a trading area are open to everyone.

  • 1:30 PM

    Rounds 4 to 7

    Pairings are posted at the front of the hall and on the event board.

  • 5:30 PM

    Top 8 playoff

    Single elimination matches on the main stage.

  • 7:30 PM

    Awards ceremony

    The champion receives the PlaneClash Trophy.
